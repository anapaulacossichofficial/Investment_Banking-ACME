Interactions

1Banker asks the agent to prepare for a meeting with a named Account.
2The agent resolves the Account and asks for confirmation, disambiguating duplicate names via location details.
3Actions retrieve approved, structured CRM context (Account, Contact, Opportunity, Task) once the Account is confirmed.
4The Account-rooted retriever pulls relevant passages from linked pitch decks, relationship notes, and research documents.
5The Prompt Template merges structured CRM facts with retrieved document evidence, comparing current CRM data against document evidence.
6Guardrails check evidence sufficiency, refuse ranking/investment-advice requests, and trigger a stated fallback when CRM data or documents are unavailable.
7The Einstein Trust Layer applies masking and audit logging before the cited, grounded briefing returns to the banker.
